# Plumbline operations report: October 2026

**Period:** 2026-10-01 00:00 UTC to 2026-10-31 23:59 UTC
**Published:** 2026-11-02

## Summary

October was a stable month for the faucet and Calix, both well above target. The SP test target (t0143103) went through a planned storage-server maintenance window from October 12 to 16. The work took longer than expected, and the target was offline for the whole window. All 1,285 sectors came back with no data loss, and the miner has produced blocks normally since October 16. The runbook now covers this failure mode and the announcement rule for future windows.

The status-page notice went up during the window rather than 24 hours before it, so under SLO.md §2.2 the window is counted as downtime. The SP figure for October is reported in full below. No network upgrade landed in October.

## Incidents

- **2026-10-01, about 13:00 to 17:03 UTC: SP test target stopped producing blocks.** Curio lost its machine registrations. 41 failed probes (about 3h 25m, roughly 47% of the 30-day budget). Post-mortem: [incidents/2026-10-01-t0143103-block-production.md](./incidents/2026-10-01-t0143103-block-production.md).
- **2026-10-12 06:30 to 2026-10-16 19:15 UTC: planned storage-server maintenance, SP test target offline.** 1,285 probes (about 107 hours). Sectors recovered in full. Write-up: [incidents/2026-10-12-t0143103-storage-outage.md](./incidents/2026-10-12-t0143103-storage-outage.md), published with this report.
- Otherwise isolated single-probe failures, none longer than 5 minutes. Faucet and Calix were not affected by either SP event.

## Faucet usage

- Drips sent: **22 tFIL drips totalling 74,000 tFIL, and 15 USDFC drips totalling 24,300 USDFC.** Every amount was read from the on-chain transaction on Blockscout; none were inferred.
- Requests came from 15 distinct IPs. 5 drip requests were rate-limited (HTTP 429): 2 on the web/API path and 3 on the public no-captcha path. 7 public-path requests were rejected as invalid input (HTTP 400), and 1 tFIL drip request failed with HTTP 500.
- No drip was refused for low reserve. Dispenser balances on 2026-11-02: about 1.44 million tFIL and 42,185 USDFC, against floors of 50,000 tFIL and 20,000 USDFC.
- Two public tFIL drips (1,000 tFIL each) were sent to the zero address `0x000...000`. It is testnet value, but the public path should reject that address. A fix is planned for November (below).
- Unauthenticated `GET` requests to drip URLs (crawlers, people opening API links in a browser) are rejected with 404 as intended and are not counted as failures.

## Drip latency note

Same measurement as August and September: server-side end-to-end time of successful `POST /api/drip/{fil,usdfc}` requests. The faucet answers only after it sees the receipt, so this is an upper bound on time to inclusion (SLO.md §2.1). The 12 tFIL drips took 43 to 89 s end to end. Every authenticated drip that passed the rate limits succeeded except the one HTTP 500 above. Rate limits are set deliberately and are not being changed.
